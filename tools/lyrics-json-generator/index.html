Simulation Assumptions

  • Drawn tiles are never discarded.
  • Played tiles leave the player's hand.
  • Held tiles remain in the player's 50-tile inventory.
  • A line occurrence is separate even when lyric text is duplicated.
  • A completed line immediately frees its active row.
  • The simulated player attempts to play every usable tile.
  • Scoring rules are intentionally excluded until finalized.
