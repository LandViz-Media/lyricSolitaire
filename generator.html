Source and catalog rules

Every lyric source keeps the order Artist, Song Title, Album, Year, Genre. Album art is currently a 200×200 thumbnail. The catalog is treated as a derived manifest: rebuilding it from the complete local library prevents sequential song generation from dropping earlier additions.
